Privacy Policy — AuraClean Studio

Last updated: October 10, 2026

1. Your Audio Files Stay Yours

AuraClean Studio processes your audio and video files entirely on your own device (in your browser). Your files are never uploaded to our servers, never stored by us, and never shared with third parties. When you close the page, nothing of your files remains with us.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not create accounts, we do not ask for your name or email, and we do not track you across the web. There is no analytics SDK embedded in the studio that identifies you personally.

3. Advertising (Google AdSense)

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Local Storage

Your DSP settings and preferences may be saved in your browser's local storage so the studio remembers them next visit. This data never leaves your device. You can clear it anytime via your browser settings.

5. Children's Privacy

This service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated date above will be revised. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: contact us via the support link on the AuraClean Studio homepage.
